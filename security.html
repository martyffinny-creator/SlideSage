What we promise

Our Security Commitments

Four non-negotiable principles that govern how we handle every file you upload.

🗑️
Data Deleted After Analysis
Your uploaded presentations are automatically and permanently deleted within 24 hours of analysis. We don't store slides longer than necessary — ever. No backups, no archives, no exceptions.
24-Hour Auto-Deletion
🔐
Encrypted In Transit & At Rest
All data moving between your browser and our servers is protected by TLS 1.3 — the latest and most secure transport protocol. Files stored temporarily are encrypted at rest using AES-256, the same standard used by financial institutions.
TLS 1.3 · AES-256
🚫
No Training on Your Data
Your slides are analysed once, the results returned to you, and that's it. We never use your presentation content to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI model — ours or anyone else's. Your intellectual property stays yours.
Zero AI Training
🏗️
SOC2-Ready Infrastructure
We build on infrastructure providers that have achieved or are pursuing SOC2 Type II certification. Netlify and Supabase both maintain rigorous internal controls, independent audits, and comprehensive security programmes.
SOC2 Ready

Built on trusted foundations

Our Infrastructure

We partner with best-in-class infrastructure providers — each with enterprise-grade security, independent audits, and proven track records.

🌐
Netlify — Hosting & Edge Delivery
SlideSage's frontend is served via Netlify's global CDN with 285+ edge nodes. Netlify provides enterprise-grade DDoS mitigation, automatic HTTPS, and instant rollbacks. All traffic is routed through hardened infrastructure with zero single points of failure.
Global CDN DDoS Protection ISO 27001 GDPR Compliant
🐘
Supabase — Database & Auth
User data is stored in Supabase's managed PostgreSQL with row-level security (RLS) enabled on every table. This means even if an attacker gained database access, they could only see rows their authenticated session permits. All connections are encrypted, and Supabase maintains detailed audit logs.
Row-Level Security PostgreSQL SOC2 Type II Encrypted Connections
🤖
OpenAI — AI Analysis Engine
Presentation analysis is powered by OpenAI's API under their Enterprise Data Protection Agreement (DPA). This legally binds OpenAI to not use API-submitted data for model training. Your slide content is never stored by OpenAI beyond the duration of the API call — typically milliseconds.
Enterprise DPA No Training on API Data Zero Data Retention SOC2 Type II

Privacy regulation

GDPR Compliance

SlideSage is designed with GDPR principles baked in from day one. We follow data minimisation, purpose limitation, and storage limitation — not just as legal requirements, but as core product values.

Questions about your data rights? Email privacy@slidesage.io

✓
Right to Access Request a full export of all data we hold about you at any time.
✓
Right to Erasure Delete your account and all associated data with a single click — no waiting period, no support ticket required.
✓
Data Minimisation We collect only what's strictly necessary to provide the service. No tracking pixels, no behavioural profiling.
✓
Lawful Basis for Processing All data processing activities have a documented lawful basis under GDPR Article 6.
✓
EU Data Residency Primary data storage is in EU regions. Cross-border transfers (e.g., OpenAI) are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses.
✓
Breach Notification We will notify affected users and relevant supervisory authorities within 72 hours of discovering a breach.
🔍

Responsible Disclosure

Found a security vulnerability in SlideSage? We appreciate the work of security researchers and take all reports seriously. Please reach out to us before public disclosure — we aim to acknowledge reports within 24 hours and resolve critical issues within 72 hours.

We do not currently offer a bug bounty programme, but we do publicly acknowledge responsible disclosures in our security changelog.
