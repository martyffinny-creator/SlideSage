🔒 Security

Security at SlideSage

Enterprise-grade protection for your presentations. Your data is handled with the highest standards of privacy, integrity, and compliance.

Our Security Commitments

Five pillars that define how we protect your data every day.

⏱️

Data Deleted After 24h

Uploaded presentation files are automatically and permanently purged from our servers within 24 hours of processing. We retain nothing longer than necessary.

✓ Automatic purge
🔐

TLS 1.3 Encryption

All data in transit is protected by TLS 1.3 — the latest and most secure transport layer standard — ensuring your files are never exposed in transit.

✓ In transit & at rest
🚫

No AI Training on Your Data

Your presentations are never used to train or fine-tune any AI model. Your intellectual property stays yours — completely and permanently.

✓ Zero training use
🛡️

SOC 2 Ready

SlideSage is built on infrastructure and practices aligned with SOC 2 Type II requirements. Our formal audit process is underway.

✓ Audit in progress
🇪🇺

GDPR Compliant

We respect EU data protection rights including the right to access, rectification, and erasure. Our DPA is available on request for business customers.

✓ EU compliant

Found a vulnerability? We want to hear from you.

🔍 Report a Security Issue

If you discover a potential security vulnerability in SlideSage, please report it responsibly. We commit to acknowledging all reports within 48 hours and resolving verified issues promptly. We will never take legal action against good-faith researchers.

Please include: description, steps to reproduce, potential impact, and any proof-of-concept (no live exploitation).
