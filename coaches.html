The Coach's Dilemma

Before they can work on delivery,
they have to fix their slides.

You're a skilled coach — but you keep getting derailed by the same basic problems session after session.

⏱️

Session time wasted on basics

You spend the first half of every session identifying slide mistakes that should have been caught beforehand. That's time you're not coaching delivery, story, or strategy.

🙈

Clients can't see their own blind spots

Your clients are too close to their material. They can't objectively evaluate their own slides — so they show up not realizing what needs work. It falls on you to break the news.

📉

No objective benchmark to track progress

It's hard to demonstrate ROI to clients or sponsors when improvement is subjective. You need an objective, repeatable score to show how far they've come.

How SlideSage Helps

Your AI-powered prep partner

Four ways SlideSage makes every coaching session more valuable — before it even starts.

1

📚 Pre-session homework

Send clients to SlideSage before every session. They arrive with AI feedback already in hand — they've seen their scores, read the recommendations, and ideally fixed the easy stuff. You skip straight to the meaningful work.

2

📊 Objective baseline

Score their deck on day one of your engagement. Then score it again at the midpoint and at the end. Now you have concrete data: their clarity score went from 54 to 87. That's a coaching ROI story that speaks for itself.

3

✅ Concrete to-dos

The AI identifies specific, actionable fixes — overcrowded slides, weak headlines, missing call to action. You focus on high-value coaching: narrative arc, executive presence, delivery. Let the AI handle the diagnostic checklist.

4

📄 Client reports

Share the PDF report with clients as a tangible deliverable from each session. It reinforces your coaching, gives them something to act on between sessions, and positions you as someone who brings structure and rigor to the work.
