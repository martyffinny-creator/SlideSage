🧠 Executive Summary

Strong investor deck with key structural improvements needed

Your Q4 investor update demonstrates a clear understanding of your market opportunity and shows strong traction data. The narrative flow from problem to solution to traction is effective, and your financial projections are well-supported. However, several slides suffer from information overload that will reduce audience retention. The most critical issue is slides 6–9, where dense text competes with your key messages. Implementing the top 5 recommendations below will likely lift your score to the 92–95 range.

🎯 Top 5 Recommendations
1
Reduce text on market opportunity slides
Slides 6–9 average 87 words each — nearly 3× the recommended maximum. Break each slide into two focused slides. Use bullet points with ≤8 words per point. Let visuals carry the data.
📌 Slides 6–9 🔴 High impact
2
Add a data source to the TAM chart
Your $42B market size claim on slide 7 is visually compelling but lacks a citation. Savvy investors will immediately ask for the source. Add a small footnote with the report name and year to build instant credibility.
📌 Slide 7 🔴 High impact
3
Simplify the financials table
Slide 14 contains a 7-column financial table that is unreadable at presentation scale. Highlight your 3 key KPIs (ARR, gross margin, burn multiple) in large type and move the full table to an appendix slide.
📌 Slide 14 🟡 Medium impact
4
Standardise heading font sizes
Heading sizes vary between 28pt and 40pt across sections with no consistent logic. Choose a 3-level type hierarchy (Title: 36pt, H2: 24pt, Body: 16pt) and apply it uniformly. This alone will make the deck feel significantly more professional.
📌 All slides 🟡 Medium impact
5
Strengthen the competitive differentiation slide
Slide 11 lists competitors but doesn't clearly articulate your defensible moat. Restructure as a 2×2 matrix with your positioning in the top-right quadrant. Add a one-sentence "why we win" statement at the bottom.
📌 Slide 11 🟡 Medium impact
