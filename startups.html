The Investor Lens

What Investors Actually Look For

Before you walk in the room, know what they're scanning for. SlideSage checks every one of these — automatically.

🎯

Clear problem and market size

TAM/SAM/SOM breakdowns that demonstrate you understand the opportunity — not just that one exists.

⚡

Differentiated solution

"We're better" is not a moat. Investors want to see a defensible, specific edge that competitors can't easily copy.

📈

Proof it works

Traction numbers, growth metrics, paying customers, testimonials — evidence beats promises every time.

🧠

A fundable team

Domain expertise, relevant credentials, and complementary skills. Why are you the people to win this market?

💰

Realistic but ambitious financials

Projections that are bold enough to justify the ask, grounded enough to survive a follow-up question in the room.

🔢

A specific, justified ask

The amount, the runway it buys, and how you'll deploy it. Vague asks signal a founder who hasn't done the math.
