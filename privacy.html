Section 1

What We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve the SlideSage service. Below is a complete breakdown of what we collect and why.

Data Type Examples Basis for Collection
Presentation Files Uploaded .pptx, .ppt, .pdf files Service delivery — required to perform analysis
Account Data Email address, name (if provided) Account creation and communication
Usage Data Pages visited, features used, analysis count, upload frequency Service improvement and abuse prevention
Session Identifiers Anonymous session tokens, temporary user IDs Maintaining session state for free (no-login) users
Technical Data Browser type, OS, IP address, device type Security, fraud detection, and analytics
Payment Data Billing postal code, last 4 card digits Processed by Stripe — we never store full card details
Free / no-account users: If you use SlideSage without creating an account, we do not collect your email address. We assign you an anonymous session ID stored in your browser. Your uploaded files are still deleted within 24 hours.

We do not collect any special categories of personal data (e.g., health information, political opinions, or biometric data). We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

Section 2

How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to provide and improve SlideSage. Here is a plain-language breakdown of every use:

  • AI Analysis: Your uploaded presentation file is sent to OpenAI's API for analysis (see Section 5). The file is transmitted securely and is not stored by OpenAI beyond the request lifecycle, per our data processing agreement.
  • Delivering Results: Analysis results are stored temporarily (up to 24 hours) so you can view and share your report. After 24 hours, both the file and results are permanently deleted from our servers.
  • Service Communications: If you have an account, we may send you transactional emails (e.g., analysis complete, account notifications). We do not send marketing emails without your explicit opt-in consent.
  • Improving the Service: Aggregated and anonymised usage patterns help us identify bugs, improve UX, and prioritise features. We never use individual presentation content for model training.
  • Fraud Prevention & Security: IP addresses and session data are used to detect abuse, prevent free-tier circumvention, and protect the platform.
  • Legal Compliance: We may process or retain data where required by applicable law.
We will never sell your data. Your presentation content, email address, and usage data are never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties for marketing purposes.

We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects without human review.

Section 3

Data Storage & Security

We take security seriously. Here is how your data is stored and protected:

  • Infrastructure: SlideSage uses Supabase (hosted on AWS in the United States) for database storage. All data is stored in PostgreSQL and encrypted at rest using AES-256. Data in transit is protected using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • File Storage: Uploaded presentation files are stored in Supabase Storage (backed by S3-compatible object storage). Each file is assigned a unique, non-guessable identifier and is accessible only to authenticated requests.
  • Retention Policy: Uploaded files and their analysis results are automatically and permanently deleted 24 hours after upload. Account data is retained for as long as your account is active, or until you request deletion.
  • Access Controls: Access to production data is restricted to authorised SlideSage personnel only, using role-based access control and multi-factor authentication.
  • Server Location: All data is stored on servers located in the United States. If you are located outside the US, your data will be transferred internationally. By using SlideSage, you consent to this transfer.
  • Incident Response: In the event of a data breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you and applicable regulatory authorities within 72 hours as required by applicable law.
Automatic 24-hour deletion: Your presentation file is automatically deleted from all our systems — including backups — within 24 hours of upload. This is a hard technical limit, not a policy promise.
Section 4

Cookies

SlideSage uses a minimal number of cookies. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies. Our cookie usage is limited to what is strictly necessary for the service to function.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration Type
ss_session Maintains your session state (login status, anonymous user ID) Session (deleted on browser close) Strictly Necessary
ss_auth Authentication token for logged-in users (Supabase Auth) 7 days (refreshed on activity) Strictly Necessary
ss_prefs Stores your UI preferences (e.g., theme, report view) 1 year Functional

Because we only use strictly necessary and functional cookies, we do not display a cookie consent banner. If this changes, we will update this policy and implement appropriate consent mechanisms.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent SlideSage from functioning correctly.

Section 5

Section 6

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data. We honour these rights regardless of where you are located.

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you at any time.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request that we permanently delete your account and all associated data. We will complete deletion within 30 days and confirm in writing. Note: presentation files are already deleted within 24 hours regardless.
  • Right to Correction: If any of your personal data is inaccurate, you can update it in your account settings or contact us to correct it.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request an export of your account data (email, usage history, saved analysis results) in JSON format.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Communications: You can unsubscribe from any marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in the email, or by updating your preferences in account settings. Transactional emails related to your account cannot be opted out of while your account is active.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request that we limit how we use your data while a complaint or correction request is being resolved.
  • Right to Object: You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will honour this unless we have compelling legal grounds to continue.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: If you believe we have handled your data unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g., your national data protection authority).
To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@slidesage.io. We will respond within 30 days and will never charge you for making a request.
