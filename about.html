What We Stand For

Our values

Three principles that guide every decision we make — from feature design to how we talk to customers.

⚡

Instant Over Perfect

Good feedback delivered in 30 seconds beats perfect feedback delivered in 3 days. Speed is a feature. We optimize for it relentlessly without sacrificing quality.

🎯

Honest Feedback

We tell you what your presentation actually needs, not what you want to hear. Actionable honesty — delivered with tact — is worth infinitely more than comfortable vagueness.

🔒

Privacy First

Your presentation contains your ideas, your strategy, your competitive edge. We delete your files after analysis and will never use your content to train AI models. Ever.

How We Got Here

Our journey

🌱
Q1 2024 — Founded

SlideSage is born

Alex and Sarah met at an AI conference, bonded over terrible investor decks, and spent the next 3 months building the first prototype. The MVP could analyze 10 slides and give 5 feedback points. Rough, but promising.

🚀
Q3 2024 — Launch

Public beta reaches 500 users in 2 weeks

The Product Hunt launch exceeded all expectations. 500 users in 14 days validated the core idea. We received 400+ pieces of feedback and used every single one. Marcus joined as Head of Product the same week.

💡
Q1 2025 — GPT-4o Integration

AI quality leap: 50+ feedback points per slide

Integrating GPT-4o transformed the feedback depth from useful to extraordinary. Analysis time dropped to 30 seconds while feedback quality tripled. We hit 2,000 users and launched paid plans.

🌍
Q1 2026 — Today

5,000 users across 12 countries

SlideSage now serves sales teams, students, executives, and coaches across 12 countries. We've analyzed 50,000+ slides and have a 4.9-star average rating. We're just getting started.
