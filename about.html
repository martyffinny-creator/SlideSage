Our Story

Built for presenters who mean business

SlideSage was born from a simple frustration: great ideas fail because of poor presentations. We built the tool we always wished existed — instant expert feedback, available to everyone.

🌿

Our Mission

To make world-class presentation feedback accessible to every person, everywhere — not just those who can afford a $500/hr communications coach.

🎯

The Problem We Saw

The average professional spends 4+ hours preparing a presentation, but rarely gets meaningful feedback before it matters. Managers are too busy. Colleagues are too polite. Coaches are too expensive.

💡

The Solution We Built

SlideSage combines the analytical depth of an experienced presentation coach with the availability of a 24/7 tool. Upload your deck, get honest, actionable feedback in 30 seconds, present with confidence.

Our Values

🔍

Honesty over comfort

Polite feedback that doesn't help you improve is worthless. We give you the honest truth your colleagues won't.

⚡

Speed without sacrifice

Fast feedback doesn't have to mean shallow feedback. We've engineered for both speed and depth.

🔓

Accessible to all

Great tools shouldn't require credit cards, signups, or enterprise contracts. Start free. Always.

🔒

Privacy first

Your presentations are confidential. We process, not store. Your IP is yours.
